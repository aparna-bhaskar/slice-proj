GEMMA-4 DECODER PERFORMANCE RESULTS
===================================

1. PER-KERNEL PERFORMANCE

CPU vs Saturn RVV:
- Gate projection: approximately 17.02x
- Up projection: approximately 17.02x
- Down projection: approximately 17.01x
- Per-layer Gate: approximately 17.01x
- Per-layer projection: approximately 17.03x

These are Spike measurements.

CPU vs Gemmini:
- Synthetic standalone INT8 matmul benchmarks.
- Tested sizes from 4x4x4 to 14x32x64.
- Speedups range from approximately 2.07x to 370.50x.
- These are Verilator measurements.

The CPU/Gemmini synthetic benchmarks are not equivalent
to the full Gemma-4 decoder projection workloads.

2. TOP-LEVEL PERFORMANCE

Scalar CPU decoder region: 3,361,431,065 cycles
Saturn RVV decoder region: 250,286,066 cycles

CPU / RVV speedup: 13.43x

These are Spike decoder-region measurements, not
necessarily full token-generation latency.

3. GEMMINI INTEGRATED DECODER

Successful integrated Verilator run.

Number of reported timed stages: 23
Sum of reported stage timings: 55,381,879 cycles

Down input preparation: 29,356,533 cycles
Share of stage subtotal: 53.0%

The stage subtotal is NOT an independently measured
end-to-end decoder runtime. Some work may be unmeasured,
and diagnostic overhead may be included.

4. LIMITATIONS

- CPU/RVV measurements use Spike.
- Gemmini measurements use GemminiRocketConfig Verilator.
- Spike and Verilator cycle counts must not be directly
  divided to claim cross-backend hardware speedup.
- Benchmark shapes and measured region boundaries differ.
- Some RVV numerical deviations were previously observed.
- A directly comparable end-to-end Gemmini speedup has
  not yet been measured.

5. INTERPRETATION

The existing measurements demonstrate substantial
CPU-to-Saturn acceleration for the measured kernels
and decoder region, and strong Gemmini acceleration
for standalone INT8 matrix multiplication.

The Gemmini stage breakdown highlights data preparation
as a potentially important performance bottleneck.
